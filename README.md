# Grocery-Store-Sales-Dashboard-Power-BI-Power-Query-Excel

An interactive **Grocery Store Sales Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, and Excel data** to analyze sales, profit, quantity, customer segments, shipping modes, product categories, and regional performance.

This project transforms raw Excel data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps users understand business performance and identify patterns across sales, profit, categories, segments, payment modes, shipping methods, and geographic regions.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw Excel data into meaningful business insights using Power Query and Power BI.

Power Query was used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation, while Power BI was used to create interactive visualizations, KPI cards, filters, and trend analysis.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance and allows users to explore sales and profitability across different business dimensions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data source and dataset
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Data visualization, dashboard development, interactive reporting, and business intelligence

## 🔍 Dashboard Insights

The dashboard includes analysis of:

- Sales by customer segment

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Sales by Segment

The dashboard compares sales across the following customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

This helps understand how different customer segments contribute to overall sales.

### 2. Sales by Payment Mode

Payment methods are analyzed to understand sales distribution across:

- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Online
- Cards

This provides an overview of sales across different payment methods.

### 3. Profit by Sub-Category

The dashboard highlights profit contributions from product sub-categories such as:

- Phones
- Binders
- Accessories
- Paper
- Copiers

This helps identify product sub-categories and their contribution to overall profitability.

### 4. Profit by Ship Mode

The dashboard compares profit across different shipping methods:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

This provides insights into profitability across shipping modes.

### 5. Geographic Analysis

The state-level map provides a visual overview of sales and profit performance across the United States.

This helps users explore geographic differences in business performance.

### 6. Time-Based Analysis

Monthly and yearly visuals help identify sales and profit trends and support performance comparisons over time.

### 7. Sales by Category

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across major product categories:

- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture
